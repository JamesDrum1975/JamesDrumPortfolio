Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What video production and content creation services do you offer?

I offer video production, social media content creation, and video editing and post-production. Projects can include promotional videos, brand stories, event coverage, interviews, testimonials, short-form social clips, and editing existing footage into polished content ready to share.

2. Do you work with small businesses and local organizations?

Absolutely. I work with small businesses, organizations, and creators who want to tell their stories, showcase what they do, and connect with their audiences through video and social media content. Whether you have a specific project in mind or need help figuring out where to start, we can discuss your goals.

3. What areas do you serve?

I'm based in Southeast Michigan and available for local production projects throughout the area. I also work remotely with clients elsewhere, particularly on video editing, post-production, and content projects that can be completed using supplied footage and digital files.

4. Can you create promotional videos and social media content for my business?

Yes. I can help create videos that introduce your business, highlight your products or services, showcase your team, or share customer stories. I can also produce content for platforms such as Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, and TikTok, including short-form videos, captions, thumbnails, and platform-ready versions.

5. Can you edit footage I already have or turn a long video into shorter clips?

Definitely. I can turn existing footage, interviews, presentations, and event videos into polished finished videos or multiple shorter clips for social media. Editing can include shaping the story, improving audio, color correction, and preparing the final files for their intended use.

6. How much do video production and editing services cost?

Every project is different, so pricing depends on the scope of work, production requirements, editing needs, and final deliverables. Contact me with a little information about your project, and I'll discuss your needs and provide a quote based on the work involved.

7. How does the process work, and how do I get started?

We start by discussing your goals, audience, timeline, and project requirements. From there, we'll plan the content and agree on the deliverables. I'll create or edit the content, share it for feedback, and make revisions as agreed before delivering the finished files. To get started, use one of the contact buttons on this website and tell me a little about your project and what you're looking to accomplish.
